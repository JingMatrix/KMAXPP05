\section[Introduction]{Introduction}

\begin{frame}{Introduction}
	Neural Networks are a flexible \textbf{family} of machine learning algorithms that can adapt to supervised, unsupervised, or reinforcement learning scenarios.
	
	\vspace{0.3cm}
	
	Although the technology is old, neural networks experienced a resurgence in popularity in the 2010s thanks to:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item the emergence of data digitization and ``cloud'' computing, providing access to vast amounts of data.
		\pause
		\item the explosion of hardware computing power, particularly chips dedicated to neural networks derived from video game graphics cards.
		\pause
		\item the acceleration of scientific research in the field of artificial intelligence.
	\end{itemize}
	
\end{frame}

\end{frame}
