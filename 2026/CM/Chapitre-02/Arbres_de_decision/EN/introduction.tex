\section[Introduction]{Introduction}

\begin{frame}{Course Content}
	Decision Trees are \textbf{supervised} machine learning algorithms used to solve regression and classification problems. Their main advantage lies in their simplicity and explainability. They are also excellent candidates for \textbf{ensemble} methods such as \textbf{bagging} and \textbf{boosting}.
	
	\vspace{0.5cm}
	
	In this course, we will cover the following topics:
	\begin{itemize}
		\pause
		\item Statistical entropy.
		\pause
		\item Decision Trees for a classification problem.
		\pause
		\item Bagging method and the Random Forests algorithm.
		\pause
		\item Boosting method and the AdaBoost algorithm.
	\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
